# UX/UI audit: blackjack-trainer at 375x812 (iPhone 13 mini)

Method: Playwright (Chromium, touch, DPR 2, ?e2e=1) driving the real app on :46104. Screenshots live in `swarm/ux/`. Prefix `d-` is Midnight Felt (dark, default) and `l-` is Bone & Ink (light). No app source was edited. Every "overflow" figure is `scrollHeight - 812` measured in the browser.

Screens covered: Home, Table (bet, dealt, wrong-play overlay, settled, bet-spread chips, count-check numpad, session report), Drills menu, all 11 drills (start, in progress, answered or result), Charts, Stats (3 tabs), Settings (all 7 sections open, and the collapsed view a real visitor sees), Profiles list and editor, Test kit menu and step, Field test gate. Not captured: the Count and Deck estimation result screens in light, and the Produce-TC result screen.

Note: `?e2e=1` forces every Settings/Stats section open (`CollapsibleSection.tsx:41`). I also captured the real collapsed Settings (`d-11-settings-collapsed.png`).

## P1: blocks use or hides the main action

### P1-1. The floating mute button sits on top of real controls
- Evidence: `d-31-table-bet-spread.png` (covers the left end of Deal), `d-34-session-report.png` and `l-34-session-report.png` (covers the left end of Back to Home), `d-61-tc-running.png` (covers the `−` key, which you need for a negative true count), `d-60-deck-running.png` (covers the 2.5 chip), `d-65-down-dealt.png` (covers bet chip 1), `d-64-bsl-answered.png` and `d-67-quiz-answered.png` (covers text), `d-02-drills.png`, `d-04-settings.png`, `d-05-stats.png`, `d-03-charts.png` (floats over content on every scrolling screen).
- Cause: `src/ui/app.css:3728` pins `.mute-btn` at `bottom: 76px` (fixed), a magic number that assumes a ~64px bottom bar. Any screen whose bottom stack is taller (the bet-chip row plus Deal plus Sit out, the NumPad, the report button) collides with it. The 44px disc is flush to the left edge, where the first key or chip of every full-width row lives.
- Fix: stop floating it. Render it inside the screen chrome: the table HUD row, the drill top bar (it already has a free slot opposite Back on most drills), and the Home/Settings header. Alternatively, anchor it above the real bottom bar's height via a CSS variable that each bottom bar publishes (`--bottom-stack-h`), and hide it while a NumPad or chip row is up. The point is that no control may sit under it.

### P1-2. Count drill: Start is 1.3 screens down, behind 12 settings
- Evidence: `d-40-drill-count-start.png`, `d-41-count-setup-full.png` (Start at y=1042 on an 812 screen; page overflow 302px at the first viewport and the full form is about 1100px).
- Cause: `CountDrillView.tsx:1575-1904` renders every option inline (Countdown, Length, Group size, Time per card, Next card, Timed challenge, Eyes-free, Distractions, Checkpoints, Count bias, Messy cards, Pace pressure, Voice answers) before a small, unstyled `drill-start-btn` (`app.css:873`).
- Fix:
  1. Pin Start (full-width, primary styling) to the bottom of the screen above the tab bar, the way Deal is pinned on the Table.
  2. Collapse everything beyond Length and Eyes-free into one `<details>` "Options", as Flashcards and the Quiz already do (`d-40-drill-flash-start.png` shows the pattern).
  3. Remember the last-used settings so Start is one tap.

### P1-3. Test kit menu: the choices are under a wall of text
- Evidence: `d-72-testkit-menu.png`, `d-72b-testkit-menu-full.png` (overflow 618px). The first viewport is the "Still unanswered" essay (five bullets, about 180 words). The first kit button ("Speaker check") only just peeks in at the bottom edge.
- Cause: `TestKit.tsx:203-226` puts the open-questions list and lede above the kit buttons (`:231`).
- Fix: put the kit buttons first (3-4 big full-width buttons, each with its `where` as a subtitle), and fold "Still unanswered" into a collapsed `<details>` below, or show it as a count badge ("5 open").

### P1-4. Table: End is one tap, unconfirmed, next to Peek, and ends the session
- Evidence: `d-20-table-bet.png`, `d-21-table-dealt.png`, `d-30-wrong-play-overlay.png`. A red End sits at the top right, 55x40, directly under Peek (61x40).
- Cause: `Table.tsx:754-762`, `handleEnd` at `:603`, `.end-btn` at `app.css:181-196`. At the top of the screen, eyes-free, one thumb-slip ends the shoe, and in Training mode there is no report to confirm it (see `d-26-end-session.png`, which dumps you straight to Home).
- Fix: make End a two-step (tap, then a "End session? Keep playing / End" sheet), or move it to the bottom row beside Sit out (the low-risk zone). Also enlarge it to 44px.

## P2: friction

### P2-1. Primary action and tap-target sizes below 44px in the thumb zone
- Table HUD: Peek 61x40, Repeat 76x35, Voice 63x35, End 55x40 (`app.css:181`, `:2928`, `:2981`).
- Home "All stats" link 61x15 (`app.css:3157`, `Home.tsx:75`). It is the only way to Stats, and has no padding.
- Stats tabs (Play/Drills/Progress) are 38px tall, and the range chips are 32px (`app.css:3285`, `:3246`).
- Settings and Profile checkboxes are 26px (`app.css:1775`). Drill-setup checkboxes are 22px, though the label row helps.
- Profile Edit/Duplicate buttons are about 35px (`d-70-profiles-list.png`, `ProfileEditor.tsx:188-210`).
- Fix: one rule, `min-height:44px; min-width:44px` (or `::before` hit-slop for the inline link), applied to `.tc-peek-btn, .end-btn, .repeat-btn, .voice-btn, .home-stats-link, .stats-tab, .stats-range-btn`. Enlarge `.settings-toggle` to 44px or make the whole row the label (it already is).

### P2-2. Table is a tiny-card, empty-middle layout; nothing is glanceable
- Evidence: `d-21-table-dealt.png`, `d-25-table-settled.png`. 48x68px cards (`app.css:250`) occupy the top 40% of the screen. The remaining ~50% is empty. The result ("Lose -1") is 16px plain text at the very bottom, partly under the mute disc, and the dealer total is never shown.
- Fix: scale cards by available height (about 72x100 or `clamp`) and center the hand vertically. Show the result as a large colored banner over the hands ("LOSE -1", with the dealer total), and color it good/bad. For eyes-free use, the voice narration already says it, but the visual should be readable in one second.

### P2-3. Count drill (and Mixed, Mastery) show cards at 48px in an otherwise empty screen
- Evidence: `d-50-count-running.png`: a single 48x68 card with "2 / 13" beneath it in 14px, in a 700px-tall void. A driver glancing at this reads nothing.
- Fix: give the single-card stage a `.card-xl` variant (about 120x170, rank at 3rem), with the progress in the top bar rather than under the card.

### P2-4. Drill result screens are small and have no obvious primary action
- Evidence: `d-52-count-result.png`, `d-54-pair-result.png`. "Wrong" plus a 14px line, then two same-weight, content-width buttons ("New run", "Back to Drills"). The result does not show the card sequence or what you should have counted.
- Fix: large verdict ("Wrong: you said 1, the count was -3"), one full-width primary ("New run", bottom-pinned), and "Back to Drills" as a quiet secondary. The tab bar already reaches Drills, so the secondary could go.

### P2-5. Start buttons are inconsistent: tiny, centered, same style as a secondary button
- Evidence: `d-40-drill-deck-start.png`, `-tc-`, `-ptc-` (all show a small 90-130px Start floating mid-screen, with the bottom 50% blank), versus Flashcards/Pair/Mastery which auto-start, versus Count with Start at the bottom of a long form.
- Cause: `.drill-start-btn` shares its rule with `.drill-next-btn` (`app.css:873-895`). `Start` is raised-surface, not the `u-btn-primary` gold used on Home.
- Fix: one `DrillStartBar` component (full-width, gold, pinned to the bottom, 56px) used by Count, Deck, True count, Produce TC.

### P2-6. Numpad and answer grids have no side gutter and a flush label
- Evidence: `d-61-tc-running.png`, `l-61-tc-running.png`. The keypad touches both screen edges (0px gutter), "Enter the true count" is flush left against the screen edge, and OK runs to the bottom edge. Compare the 16px gutter used by the ActionBar.
- Cause: `.numpad` (`app.css:685`) has no padding, and `TrueCountDrillView.tsx:742` places it directly in the screen.
- Fix: `padding: 0 var(--space-4) var(--space-3)` on `.numpad`; fold the label into the display field ("True count?").

### P2-7. Wrong-play card: the "BASIC STRATEGY ERROR" pill overflows the card
- Evidence: `d-30-wrong-play-overlay.png` (the pill runs past the right edge of the red card).
- Cause: `MistakeCard.tsx:169` renders `copy.label` in `.mistake-class` next to "WRONG", with no `flex-wrap` or `min-width:0`.
- Fix: allow wrap, or shorten the copy ("Basic error", "Missed index").

### P2-8. Raw ids and jargon leak into feedback
- Evidence: `d-66-flash-answered.png` shows "hard-13-v-3" and "Box 1/5 · due in 24h". The Mastery start shows "1 / 330 cleared".
- Cause: `Drills.tsx:1013` (`feedback-cell` renders `card.cellId`) and `:1020` (`formatSrCard`, `srStatus.ts:249`).
- Fix: render "Hard 13 vs 3" for the cell (a `labelForCell()`), and replace "Box 1/5 · due in 24h" with "Next review: tomorrow". Both are tiny 11-12px lines the operator will not use mid-drive anyway; consider hiding them behind the Options disclosure.

### P2-9. Quiz feedback pushes Next below the fold
- Evidence: `d-67-quiz-answered.png` (overflow 97px). The correction card (WRONG, YOU PLAYED / CORRECT, explanation, Show on chart) plus the Index picker and Eyes-free/Voice row above it exceed the screen, so Next is below the fold. The mute disc also overlays the explanation text.
- Fix: collapse the Index/Eyes-free/Voice/Options row once a question is on screen (it is setup, not play), or pin Next in the bottom bar like Flashcards does.

### P2-10. Home: dead space, truncated profile name, test tooling at the same weight as play
- Evidence: `d-01-home.png`. The whole column is centered (`justify-content:center`, `app.css:49`), leaving about 270px empty above and below. The profile chip reads "Default (6D ..." (truncated at 22rem, `app.css:86-100`), so you cannot tell which ruleset is active, which matters (H17 vs S17). The three readiness labels are 9.9px. "Test kit" is a second full button directly under "Play a shoe" for what is a diagnostics tool (it already has an entry in Settings > Tests).
- Fix: top-align content; put "Play a shoe" and a "Drills" shortcut (the last-used drill, "Resume Flashcards") at the bottom thumb zone above the tab bar; show the full profile name on its own line under the title (or shorten the default name to "6D H17"); drop the Test kit button from Home (Settings > Tests already links to it); raise label font to 11px minimum.

### P2-11. Light theme contrast failures

| Drills group headings | 3.23 | 10.9px | `app.css` `.drills-group-title` |

### P2-12. Settings: the first section is long prose, and the rest are hidden behind it
- Evidence: `d-11-settings-collapsed.png`. The only open section ("In the car", `Settings.tsx:224`) is 700px tall because of two paragraphs of explanation, so the Theme/Play/Audio headings are at or past the fold, and the mute disc overlays the "Tests" header. `d-10-settings-full.png`: the Shot clock hint (below "Shot clock") renders at body size, far larger than the other notes.
- Fix: cut the two explanatory paragraphs under "The two wheel buttons" to one line (move the detail behind a "?" disclosure). Make the Shot-clock note use the standard `u-note` class. Consider "In the car" collapsed with its three controls as a summary line.

### P2-13. "Back to Home" duplicated everywhere with a persistent Home tab
- Evidence: top-left Back buttons on Settings, Stats, Profiles, Charts, and bottom buttons on Drills and Session report.
- Cause: `Settings.tsx:206`, `Stats.tsx:630`, `ProfileEditor.tsx:176`, `Drills.tsx:2572`, `Table.tsx:273`. The tab bar (C4) was added to make these redundant.
- Fix: remove them on tab destinations (Settings, Charts, Drills, Stats). Keep a single "Back" only where it returns to a parent (Profile editor, Stats from Home). This also removes the competing bottom button on the Drills menu, which collides with the mute disc.

## P3: polish

- P3-1. Tab bar labels at 9.9px (`primitives.css:180`) and glyph icons that don't say what the tab is (diamond for Home). Use 11px and real icons, or at least larger labels.
- P3-2. "Play" tab vs "Play a shoe" button vs Table heading vs "Test mode": pick one name. Also "Drills" (menu title) vs drill names such as "Count drill" and "True count drill" vs "Produce the true count" (the last is a different thing from "True count drill" and the descriptions are the only way to tell).
- P3-3. Session report (`d-34-session-report.png`): the h1 is far larger than every other screen title; the "Back to Home" button and tab bar both show; the mistakes list has no visual grouping. Use the standard top-bar title and a gold primary.
- P3-4. Drills menu (`d-02-drills.png`): the top of the screen fits only 5 of 11 drills. Nothing says which to do next or what is "due". Add a "Continue: <last drill>" card on top.
- P3-5. Table disabled actions (`d-21-table-dealt.png`): "Split" is dark-on-dark and illegible (needed at a glance to know why it is off). Hide illegal actions entirely when there are fewer than 5, or use a 40% opacity of the normal label, not a darker surface. "Surrender" text touches the button edges at 0.8rem (`app.css:484`).
- P3-6. Downswing (`d-40-drill-down-start.png`): the instruction paragraph is four lines pressed against the chip row, and the first half of the screen is empty. Put it in the top third.
- P3-7. Deck estimation start (`d-40-drill-deck-start.png`): jargon "Resolution: Half / Last deck / Quarter" and a paragraph in the middle of the screen; Produce-TC start has no explanation at all (`d-40-drill-ptc-start.png`).
- P3-8. Charts (`d-03-charts.png`): the Row order control, legend (8 codes) and explainer take the whole first screen; the actual chart starts at y~1080/1624 and is cut. Collapse "Cell codes" behind a disclosure, and move "Row order" into a small segmented control in the header.
- P3-9. Stats first screen (`d-05-stats.png`): the four tabs plus five range chips plus status line take about 220px of the first screen before any data. Collapse the range chips into one dropdown. The CVCX rows show "-" in an empty profile; hide empty rows.
- P3-10. Field test gate (`d-74-fieldtest-gate.png`): a roughly 250-word paragraph at 12.5px above Start, plus a 49-step list. Start (about y=640) is visible, but nothing here is readable in a car. Default to a 2-line lede with the rest behind "Details".
- P3-11. Light theme: the Home profile chip, tab bar and cards read fine. No other overlap found that is not already listed.

## Top 10 (what to fix first)
1. Mute disc covers real controls (P1-1).
2. Count drill Start below the fold behind 12 settings (P1-2).
3. Test kit menu: buttons under a wall of text (P1-3).
4. Table End: one tap, unconfirmed, beside Peek (P1-4).
5. Sub-44px targets: HUD buttons, All stats, Stats tabs, profile buttons (P2-1).
6. Table and Count drill: tiny cards, empty screen, small result (P2-2, P2-3).
7. Light theme contrast: "Highest first" chip 2.41, tab labels, build, group titles (P2-11).
8. Start buttons: inconsistent, small and unstyled (P2-5), together with result screens lacking a primary (P2-4).
9. Home: dead space, truncated profile, Test kit at Play's weight (P2-10).
10. Quiz feedback pushes Next off-screen, and the wrong-play pill overflows (P2-9, P2-7).
