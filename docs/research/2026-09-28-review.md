# Review of the field-test protocol and the ten branches — 2026-09-28

Four independent read-only reviews (end-to-end process; route branches §3/§4/§7/§10 + §0.1/§0.3;
lock and wheel branches §1/§5/§9/§2/§6/§8 + §0.2/§0.4; honesty audit of every test added on
2026-09-27), reconciled here. "Verified" means the reviewer traced the code path and I re-read the
lines; "suspected" means the mechanism is real but the iOS behaviour it depends on is not proven.

## Verdict

The core measurement — the route 2×3 crossing, the microphone gates, the wheel block, navigation,
persistence and the settings snapshot — traced clean end to end. Nothing found corrupts route or
wheel data.

The steps added on 09-27 are where the defects are: the **lock probe can produce a false or wrong
verdict** four different ways, **`free` no longer stays put**, the **car's own `play`/`pause` are
counted as wheel presses**, and the doc's car-park gates for §3/§4/§10 would reject clean legs.

## Status after round 1 (2026-09-28)

B1–B4 and S1 fixed and deployed. The marker is set on `visibilitychange: hidden` (not on
arrival), cleared on `visible`, on leaving the step, and on a `pagehide` with
`persisted=false` (a navigation announces itself; a kill fires nothing — and the spec order is
`pagehide` → `hidden`, so a hidden that arrives while unloading is ignored). The verdict is
scored on the largest gap between lock, ticks and unlock: `normal` ≤ 2.5 ticks; `frozen` ≥ half
the window; `throttled` between. Tick rows are written only while hidden, capped at 60 per
window; the result carries `maxGapMs`, `ticksInside`, `focusPlayingAtHidden/AtVisible`.
`lock-probe` sits before `free`. The wheel probe counts only the four press actions;
`play`/`pause`/`stop`/`seekto` write `wheel field-test-transport` and count as nothing; every
arrival carries `focusPlaying`.

## Status after round 3 (2026-09-28)

Round 2's review found no new blockers; what follows is what it corrected.

- **Run state** — a condition change or a reload that leaves the pointer on a step no longer
  on the path (a wheel step after switching to `speakerphone`) snaps it to the next on-path
  step. `armedProbes` is coerced from storage, so a resumed run keeps its probes.
- **Lock probe** — the last tick before `hidden` is the start of the window: iOS can deliver
  `hidden` on the way back, and a 300 ms window with a tick 30 s before it is `frozen`, not
  `too-short`. Ticks are kept while the screen is on (bounded at 600 then; since round 7
  only the last two, which are all the window's start needs); `pagehide` writes
  `lock-probe-pagehide persisted=`; the meaningless `expected` field is gone and
  `gapBeforeHiddenMs` replaces it.
- **Ambient** — the five-second window opens by itself when the instruction has been read
  (arrival only), and chimes when the reading lands or fails. Measure remains for a second
  reading. A read-aloud abandoned by leaving the step no longer leaves `reading` up.
- **S4 leftovers** — one `stepWantsVoice`; `spokenAnswersLive` is false on
  `transcriptIsEvidence` steps and gates `isAttempt` and `onTranscript`; the banner says
  "what you say is the evidence here" on `mic-heard`.
- **Voice options** — one `spokenOpts(audio)` (rate, voiceURI, effective volume) at all four
  speaking sites; the pause cue and the lock verdict had dropped `volume`.
- **Note** — one writer, carrying `run=`; saved before Finish and Pause tear the context down.
- **Hold** — see S9 above: live TTS re-asserts, never holds.
- **F5 / F7, answered** — `armedProbes` stays per run: a probe armed on one leg is a step on
  that leg's path, and `setFieldTestCondition` keeping it is the same rule as keeping the
  stamps. No-Bluetooth legs have no `input-selected` row: the probe runs at `mic-settled` on
  `wheel-with-mic`, which is off their path; expected, not a gap.
- **Tests** — arm-once now has a non-uniform second scoring (it could not fail before);
  MediaSession `play` is tested under the probe and after a release; the live-TTS re-hold
  test holds first and a no-holder case asserts no element; `run-start steps=` is asserted
  under `speakerphone`; the classifier has the late-`hidden` case.

## Status after round 5 (2026-09-28)

Round 4's review found one blocker and the rest nits; all done.

- **Blocker: the "froze late" rule was bypassed by the overdue tick.** One tick landing on
  resume before `visible` (the module's own premise) made the last gap small, so a page that
  kept cadence for two-thirds of the window and then froze read `throttled`. The rule is now
  one over-cadence gap with at most one tick after it. The window start moves back one tick
  only when the last tick before `hidden` is that overdue callback (over-cadence gap ahead of
  it, landed within a cadence of the event), so an older stall with the cadence back after it
  cannot drag the start into it.
- **Gate row to read** — §3 names the last `why=step-open` row before the step's final
  `answer` (a re-opened step writes a fresh one), and the `stateAtArrival` whitelist is
  `listening|restarting|starting`.
- **Rows** — `condition-changed` and `answer-by-voice` carry `run=` only while the run has
  not ended: on the gate after a Finish they belong to the next leg. The dead fallbacks on the
  arrival snapshot are gone.
- **Tests** — the overdue-tick variant of the late freeze and the stale-stall case;
  `speak()`'s clip path after a release; the `focus restart why=play-request` row;
  `condition-changed run=` present mid-run and absent after Finish; `probesOnPath=false` on a
  cell with no site; `run=` on the boot row; a second lock on the same step reads a clean
  baseline; the "leaving a measurement" e2e now leaves for the next step and reads the stale
  `measuring` off that step's "Say it again" (the Pause/Resume copy, which remounted and
  could not fail, is gone).
- **Round 6, from the round-5 review (no blockers)** — the read-aloud instruction is in the

  7), so an echo off the car could stamp the step and start the after block before the press

  answers and stamps. The conditional `run=` on the gate rows (`condition-changed`,

  between runs as often as inside one; `run-start`/`run-resume` now carry `answerByVoice`,
  which is what a run actually ran with. §3/§10 say what `off` means on a re-opened step
  and use one whitelist; §7's straddled-block verdict corrected; the switch's step count is
  seven (`mic-heard` takes no spoken answer); stale comments on `setFieldTestAnswerByVoice`,
  `pauseFieldTestRun` and an orphaned settings-snapshot comment removed. `speak()`'s live
  path is `speakAsyncLive` now (one live path): its own `speakLive` kept no reference to the
  utterance and had no watchdog, so the round-4 re-assert hung on its `onend` could never fire

- **Round 7, from the round-6 review (one blocker)** — a frozen page under a lock of 4–5 s

  half), so a span with no tick in it had `maxGap == span <= allowed`. The bound is now the
  allowed gap. The tick array is no longer capped at 600: only the last two ticks matter
  while the screen is on, and the cap shifted them out under a lock longer than twenty
  minutes (a normal one read `throttled`, past forty `frozen`). The arrival and each verdict
  seed the next window's first tick, so a frozen re-lock with a late `hidden` reads
  `frozen`, not `too-short`. "skip" is no longer a synonym for `missed`: `wheel-with-mic`

  records the close, so a leg re-opened as no-Bluetooth resumes on `route-after-mic` with

  `run-start`/`run-resume`. The duplicate mic-step spoken-answer e2e is gone; §0.1 marked

- **Round 8, from the round-7 review (no blockers)** — the lock e2e that jump the clock
  while the page is "visible" dispatch the jump and the events in one turn of the page: a

  verdicts a coin toss and could let the seed's test pass without the seed. The explicit

- **Round 11, the three gaps that were left as noted, now built** — (1) the lock marker is
  armed when the step OPENS (`{session, onStepAt, hiddenAt?}`), not when the page goes hidden,
  so a page killed under a `hidden` iOS never delivered leaves something behind: a dead
  session's marker with no `hiddenAt` scores `unloaded-no-hidden`, which says "the app died on

  reading from a leg that never reached the step, which were the same silence. The marker is
  cleared when the step is left (with the screen on or off) and on `pagehide persisted=false`.
  (2) `resumeFieldTestRun` puts the microphone's close time back when nothing else could: a
  reload or a kill runs no cleanup, and the pointer can have moved while the run was away (the
  condition picker sits beside Resume, and a leg with no Bluetooth has no wheel steps), so a
  run interrupted on `wheel-with-mic` came back on `route-after-mic` exporting every after

  so the offsets are lower bounds. (3) The half-typed note is written to

  gate, beside the lock probe's own boot scoring, for the reason that effect already gives:

  back in the box when the run resumes onto the same step of the same run under the same
  condition, so the sentence can be finished; what is typed after that is saved on the way
  out as its own row. Its own key rather than the run store because `write` there persists
  the whole run blob, notifies every subscriber, and is cross-tab synced -- a keystroke would
  be a storage event in every other copy of the app.
- **Round 12, the review of round 11.** The marker shape `{hiddenAt, session}` that the build
  IN THE CAR writes is read rather than dropped, with `hiddenAt` standing in for `onStepAt`
  (it is true of it) -- that build sets the marker only on `hidden`, so the marker it leaves
  behind is a page that died under a lock, and requiring `onStepAt` threw it away on exactly
  the boot where it exists. The back-filled microphone close time is now MARKED
  (`mic-close-backfilled {from: touchedAt, to}`): it is a lower bound, and read against
  samples at 1.5/9/20/31 s a phone resumed an hour later exports its first answer at two
  seconds, which a reader files as the 1.5 s point. Both `note` writers flush before dropping
  the draft key (`diag` buffers for a second, and that text has already survived one death).
  The box's save buffer starts EMPTY even when the box does not: restored text is already a
  `recovered` row, and React's development double-invoke ran the exit-save cleanup at mount,
  which filed the sentence early and left a box whose Save did nothing. Three docblocks that
  still stated the pre-round-11 invariant ("the marker goes down when the page goes hidden")
  were rewritten -- `FieldTest.tsx`, `lockProbe.ts` and `e2e/field-test-lock.spec.ts` -- since
  a file that specifies the opposite of what it does is how the change gets reverted. Tests:
  the draft store's field checks and its untrimmed return, `resumeFieldTestRun`'s four
  branches at the store (the screen clears the value on its first render, so only the store
  can see the one that matters), the recovered row landing at the gate with nothing tapped,
  and the run-id and condition halves of the seed predicate -- each pinned by a mutation that
  used to survive.
- **Left as noted, not built** — Start with the pointer on step 1 after a
  Back offers no Resume (older; one tap discards the run's answers, the log rows survive). And
  a SECOND COPY OF THE APP OPEN AT THE GATE can score the driving copy's marker as
  `unloaded-no-hidden` and clear it, leaving the live tab unarmed until its next unlock: the

  Related, and honest rather than wrong: a kill on the step after a lock was already scored
